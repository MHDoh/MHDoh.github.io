\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Wireless Networks Laboratory, Koç University}{Istanbul, Türkiye}
      {Graduate Research Assistant, TÜBİTAK 1711 AI-P5G project with Turkcell and Opticoms}{Oct 2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Safe hierarchical RL for O-RAN [M1].} Three PPO agents on two O-RAN timescales set slice PRB/power budgets
        and schedule URLLC and eMBB users; an execution-time safety layer built on conformal demand bounds and conservative
        channel-rate bounds corrects allocations before they are applied. Versus fixed schedulers: 61\% fewer URLLC deadline
        misses; worst-user eMBB rate raised from 1.95 to 2.49\,Mbps.
  \item \textbf{Lightweight CSI prediction [J1].} GRU encoder with Luong attention, gated fusion, and a factorized output head;
        26\% fewer parameters and $2.3\times$ the throughput of LinFormer at $-13.84$\,dB NMSE on
        3GPP TR~38.901 channels.
  \item \textbf{Applied private-5G control (factory site, Ankara).} Developed a controller package for the project's commercial
        LiteOn FlexFi gNB that lowers configured transmit power while protecting device connectivity and cell service: a data
        pipeline over DU statistics, PM counters, and walk-test logs; a guarded neural power selector with NETCONF actuation;
        and a digital twin that replays recorded walks to compare policies with fixed and rule-based baselines.
        Evaluated offline and prepared for factory integration.
\end{itemize}
